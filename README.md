\# GlowGrid(Skincare Product, Routines and Remainder Management System)

\## Overview

GlowGrid is a skincare products, routines, and reminder management system designed to help users organize and manage their skincare activities in one place. The system aims to make skincare management simple, organized, and consistent.

\## Scope

\- In scope: Add skincare products.

\- In scope: Adding multiple products to a routine.

\- In scope: Notifications for scheduled routines.

\- Out of scope: Online purchasing of skincare products.

\- Out of scope: Online doctor appointments or consultations.

\## Stakeholders

\- Skincare Users: Want to organize products, create routines, receive reminders, and track their skincare activities easily.

\- Skincare Product Brands: May benefit from accurate product information and visibility of their products within the system.

\- System Administrator: Wants to manage users, system data, product information, and maintain smooth system operation.

\## Functional requirements

\- FR-01: Product Management: The system shall allow users to add, edit, view, and delete skincare products.

\- FR-02: Routine Management: The system shall allow users to create morning and evening skincare routines and arrange products in their preferred order.

\- FR-03: Reminder \& Tracking: The system shall allow users to set reminders, receive notifications, and mark routines as completed or missed.

\## Nonfunctional requirements

\- NFR-01: Usability: The system shall have a simple and user-friendly interface that new users can easily understand.

\- NFR-02: Reliability: The system shall save users’ product and routine information accurately and provide reminders at scheduled times, subject to device notification settings.

\- NFR-03: Performance: The system shall respond quickly when users add, edit, view, or delete products and routines.

\## Acceptance criteria

\- FR-01:Successful Product Management: Users should be able to add, edit, view, categorize, and delete skincare products without difficulty.

\- FR-02:Successful Routine Creation: Users should be able to create personalized morning, evening, and custom skincare routines and assign products to them.

\- FR-03:Reliable Reminders: The system should successfully generate reminders according to the schedules set by users.
